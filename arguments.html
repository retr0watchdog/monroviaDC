Take Action

Which Side of the Fight Are You Interested In?

There are many ways to help protect Morgan County from this harmful data center development. Choose the area that matches your interests and skills to make the biggest impact in our fight.

Every person who gets involved makes our community stronger!
🌱

Environmental

The data center threatens our environment through massive water consumption, air pollution from backup generators, noise pollution that disrupts wildlife, and industrial development destroying farmland and natural habitat.

How You Can Help:

  • Monitor and document environmental impacts
  • Attend environmental impact hearings
  • Connect with environmental advocacy groups
  • Research water usage data and air quality concerns
  • Document noise pollution in your area
Get Involved in Environmental Action
⚖️

Legal

Legal action is a key battleground for protecting Morgan County against this data center. We're fighting Google and developers who are cutting corners in construction and operation, violating environmental regulations, and pushing through approvals without proper oversight.

How You Can Help:

  • Support our legal defense fund
  • Help research zoning and environmental law violations
  • Document permit violations or procedural irregularities
  • Connect with legal professionals willing to help
  • Attend zoning and planning meetings
Support Legal Defense Fund Join Legal Research Team
📊

Business & Economics

The data center threatens our local economy by consuming massive resources while providing few permanent jobs, straining infrastructure that taxpayers must upgrade, and reducing property values in surrounding areas.

How You Can Help:

  • Research economic impact studies
  • Analyze tax incentive packages and their true costs
  • Connect with local business owners opposed to the project
  • Document infrastructure costs borne by taxpayers
  • Research property value impacts from similar projects
Join Economic Analysis Team
🗳️

Political

Political action is where we elect representatives that actually protect us instead of serving corporate interests. We need officials who will stand up for communities over profit margins.

How You Can Help:

  • Contact your representatives about the data center
  • Attend town halls and public meetings
  • Research candidate positions on development issues
  • Organize voter registration drives
  • Support candidates who prioritize community protection
Get Involved in Political Action Find Political Events

Human Health

The data center threatens public health through air pollution from diesel generators, water contamination risks, noise pollution affecting mental health, and increased truck traffic creating safety hazards for families and children.

How You Can Help:

  • Document health impacts from noise and air pollution
  • Connect with healthcare professionals
  • Research health studies from similar facilities
  • Monitor air and water quality
  • Advocate for health impact assessments
Join Health Advocacy Team
📢

Civic Action / Outreach

Community outreach is essential for building the grassroots movement needed to stop this project. We need people to spread awareness, organize neighbors, and build the coalition necessary to protect our community.

How You Can Help:

  • Canvass neighborhoods to inform residents
  • Organize community meetings and events
  • Manage social media campaigns
  • Create and distribute flyers and materials
  • Coordinate volunteer activities
Join Outreach Team Find Volunteer Opportunities
